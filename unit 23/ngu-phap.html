📐 Grammar & Functional Structures — Unit 3

1. Đưa lời khuyên với Should / Shouldn't + Lý do

Cấu trúc: S + should / shouldn't + V (bare-infinitive) + because...

• Students should listen when others speak because it shows respect.

• You shouldn't interrupt people because it can make them feel uncomfortable.

2. Lời yêu cầu lịch sự (Polite Requests)

Mức độ Cấu trúc Ví dụ Mẫu câu
Cơ bản Can you + V...? Can you open the door?
Lịch sự Could you + V, please? Could you help me with this bag, please?
Trang trọng Would you mind + V-ing...? Would you mind closing the window?

3. Bất đồng ý kiến lịch sự (Polite Disagreement)

• I understand your point, but...

• I see it differently because...
